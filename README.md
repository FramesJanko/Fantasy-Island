# Premise

The game will have a small play area with up to 8 players in it. Heavy contest for resources. Each player controls a hero. Enemies spawn and assault any nearby heroes, and a small fort with NPCs in the center by a lake. Enemies drop items that need to be identified and players level up from combat experience.

The game ends if all players die. Player(s) win if they survive a number of rounds of play. The number of rounds is determined by the number of players alive (more players alive, more rounds needed).

## Combat

Player controls are moba-like, with top down view, click to move, and abilities that are unique to their starting hero. All units, including heroes, have an auto attack. 

Heroes have stats, including attack damage, attack speed, movement speed, health, energy, strength, agility, intellect, armor, resistances to damage types.

Strength determines attack damage, health, and movement speed. Agility determines attack speed, energy, and movement speed. Intelligence determines mana and experience received.

## Leveling up

The players control one hero that starts at level 1. They can level up from experience, which is earned when units die. Exp is given to the killing blow.
